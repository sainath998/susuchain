SusuChain

Rotating savings circles (susu · tanda · chit fund · ROSCA) on Stellar, with no single treasurer. The pot lives in a Stellar account controlled by all members through multisig, and paying anyone out needs a majority of members to sign. Stellar Testnet

This creates funded testnet accounts for each member and a pool account (via Friendbot). Every member is added as a signer on the pool, and the pool's own master key is disabled, so nobody holds the pot alone.
